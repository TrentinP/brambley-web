      <div class="bo-intro-copy">
        <p>Many years ago, I was walking on the Scripps Pier in La Jolla when I
          read the story of oceanographer George McEwen. Beginning in the early
          twentieth century, McEwen kept handwritten records of ocean temperature
          and salinity off the pier. At the time, he was one of very few people
          making such observations along the Pacific Rim. Today, the data he
          collected is vital to our understanding of how the ocean is evolving.</p>
        <p>The story inspired me to think about what data I could record and
          preserve that might one day prove valuable to our understanding of how
          the Pacific Northwest is changing over time. Where could I add data
          points to areas of scientific research?</p>
